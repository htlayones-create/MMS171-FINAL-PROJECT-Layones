About Me!

Hello! Welcome! It's me Jian!

I’m a 19-year-old Multimedia Studies sophomore at UP Open University, based in Pasig City. Most days, you’ll find me working on creative ops for a local youth council or volunteering around Pasig City and our church in Antipolo.

When I’m not busy with media projects, I’m usually journaling, crafting, pulling a tarot card or two, or embracing my Capricorn side. I love solitude and quiet moments, even if I lowkey dislike feeling stuck in too much silence. At the end of the day, I just love combining structured media work with visual storytelling, keeping things purposeful and community driven.
